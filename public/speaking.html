Work About Now Seminars

University seminars

I am available for short technical talks for engineering courses and research groups in Korea. The talks draw on actual development and open-source contributions.

Suggested topics

Where simulations differ from real systems

Time steps, physical models, and sensor data when vehicle dynamics are represented by discrete computations.

Working on the Autoware–CARLA interface

Upstream fixes concerning sensor publication, timestamps, camera data, and vehicle commands.

Numerical modeling and validation

Numerical computation, model–measurement comparisons, and residual-based debugging using trajectory solvers and vehicle projects.

Format

A 30–50 minute talk followed by questions. Topics and technical depth can be adjusted for the audience. The default language is Korean.

Background

I maintain autoware_carla_interface in Autoware Universe. See code contributions, KSAE member news, and related work and coverage.
